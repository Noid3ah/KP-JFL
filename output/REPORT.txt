2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & 4-PERSON MODULE ALLOCATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project is strictly decomposed into 4 independent yet integrated modules
satisfying the project brief:

PERSON 1 — PROJECT LEAD & LEAGUE MANAGEMENT (Main Focus: League Operations)
  - Assigned Classes: FootballLeague, LeagueCommissioner, Fixture, MatchResult, MatchOfficial
  - Responsibilities:
      * Orchestrates the overall league and points table.
      * Creates fixtures assigning home/away teams, stadiums, dates, and times.
      * Assigns match officials while strictly preventing scheduling conflicts.
      * Records match scores and applies official points: Win = 3, Draw = 1, Loss = 0.
      * Integrates modules from Person 2, Person 3, and Person 4.

PERSON 2 — TEAM & PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT (Main Focus: Teams and People)
  - Assigned Classes: Person (Abstract Superclass), Player, Coach, FootballTeam
  - Responsibilities:
      * Establishes the foundational Person base class for the entire project.
      * Manages football club profiles (Harbour View, Tivoli Gardens, Portmore, etc.).
      * Enforces minimum age rules: Players must be >= 16; Coaches must be >= 21.
      * Prevents duplicate jersey numbers (1-99) within the same club squad.
      * Tracks player positions, goals, and playing status (Active, Injured, Suspended).

PERSON 3 — STADIUM & TICKET MANAGEMENT (Main Focus: Stadium Operations & Ticketing)
  - Assigned Classes: Stadium, Ticket, Spectator
  - Responsibilities:
      * Manages stadium seating capacity, physical location, and field condition.
      * Tracks which teams are assigned to each stadium for match fixtures.
      * Manages ticket categories (Bleachers, Grandstand, VIP, Ultra VIP).
      * Sells tickets to spectators and prevents sales from exceeding stadium capacity.

PERSON 4 — STADIUM SHOP, FINANCE & DATA MANAGEMENT (Main Focus: Sales, Tax & Persistence)
  - Assigned Classes: Product (Abstract Superclass), Merchandise, FoodItem, StadiumShop, DataPersistence
  - Responsibilities:
      * Establishes the Product hierarchy for match-day sales.
      * Manages club merchandise (jerseys, caps, scarves) and Jamaican food/drinks (patties, Ting, jerk chicken).
      * Processes transactions, calculates Subtotal, exactly 15% Jamaican GCT, total amount, and change.
      * Prevents transactions from completing when payment is insufficient or out of stock.
      * Handles sequential flat-file persistence (kpfl_teams.txt, kpfl_stadiums.txt, kpfl_shop_inventory.txt, kpfl_shop_receipts.txt).

3. OBJECT-ORIENTED DESIGN RATIONALE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The architecture explicitly addresses the primary pillars of OOP:

1. ENCAPSULATION:
   - All internal data members in all classes are declared `private` or `protected`.
   - Access is mediated through validated getters and mutators.
   - For example, team points cannot be directly manipulated; they only update
     through the invariant method `FootballTeam::recordMatch(gf, ga, points)`.

2. INHERITANCE:
   - Person Hierarchy (Person 2):
       Person (Abstract)
         ├── Player (specializes position, jersey number, goals)
         ├── Coach (specializes qualifications, role, experience)
         ├── MatchOfficial (specializes officiating roles, grade, availability)
         ├── LeagueCommissioner (specializes team approval, suspension, match inspection)
         └── Spectator (specializes fan loyalty, email, tickets bought)
   - Product Hierarchy (Person 4):
       Product (Abstract)
         ├── Merchandise (specializes size, team association, material)
         └── FoodItem (specializes beverage flag, expiry date, perishability)

3. POLYMORPHISM:
   - In Person: `virtual void displayInfo() const = 0;`
     Overridden polymorphically by Player, Coach, MatchOfficial, Commissioner, and Spectator.
   - In Product: `virtual void displayProduct() const = 0;`
     Overridden polymorphically by Merchandise and FoodItem.

4. ABSTRACTION:
   - Both Person and Product serve as high-level abstract interfaces. Concrete details
     are hidden from caller systems such as the StadiumShop or LeagueCommissioner.

5. RELATIONSHIPS (ASSOCIATION, AGGREGATION, COMPOSITION):
   - Composition (Strong ownership, whole-part lifecycle):
       * FootballTeam *-- Player / Coach (Team owns squad instances)
       * FootballLeague *-- Fixture (League owns fixture lifecycle via unique_ptr)
       * Fixture *-- MatchResult / Ticket (Result and tickets belong to the fixture)
       * StadiumShop *-- Product (Shop manages product memory)
   - Aggregation (Weak ownership, independent lifecycle):
       * FootballLeague o-- LeagueCommissioner
       * Fixture o-- MatchOfficial (Officials exist independently of fixtures)
   - Association:
       * Fixture --> Stadium & FootballTeam (References home team, away team, and ground)
       * Ticket ..> Spectator (Ticket records purchaser ID)

4. MANDATORY BUSINESS RULES & EXCEPTION HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project implements a cohesive, strongly-typed custom exception hierarchy
inheriting from `JFLException` (which derives from `std::runtime_error`):

  Rule 1: Unregistered or suspended teams cannot be scheduled.
          -> Throws `TeamNotEligibleException`.
  Rule 2: A team cannot play in two fixtures at the same date and time.
          -> Throws `SchedulingConflictException`.
  Rule 3: A stadium cannot host two fixtures at the same date and time.
          -> Throws `SchedulingConflictException`.
  Rule 4: A match official cannot be booked for conflicting fixtures.
          -> Throws `OfficialConflictException`.
  Rule 5: Match scores cannot be negative numbers.
          -> Throws `InvalidScoreException`.
  Rule 6: Fixture cannot be marked complete without a recorded score.
          -> Throws `ResultRequiredException`.
  Rule 7: Players must be at least 16 years old.
          -> Throws `InvalidAgeException`.
  Rule 8: Coaches must be at least 21 years old.
          -> Throws `InvalidAgeException`.
  Rule 9: No duplicate jersey numbers allowed within the same team.
          -> Throws `PersonnelException`.
  Rule 10: Ticket sales cannot exceed stadium seating capacity.
           -> Throws `CapacityExceededException`.
  Rule 11: Stadium shop transactions cannot complete if payment is insufficient.
           -> Throws `InsufficientPaymentException`.
  Rule 12: Stadium shop transactions cannot complete if items are out of stock.
           -> Throws `OutOfStockException`.

5. MATCH-DAY FINANCE & PERSISTENCE SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finance and GCT Calculation:
  - All shop purchases apply the Jamaican General Consumption Tax (GCT) at 15%:
      Subtotal = Sum of (UnitPrice * Quantity) for all purchased items
      GCT (15%) = Subtotal * 0.15
      Total Amount = Subtotal + GCT
      Change = CashTendered - Total Amount
  - If CashTendered < Total Amount, the transaction rolls back inventory and throws
    `InsufficientPaymentException`.

File Persistence (DataPersistence):
  - Uses sequential flat-text storage with delimiter '|':
      * kpfl_teams.txt: Saves team registration number, name, parish, wins, draws, losses, points.
      * kpfl_stadiums.txt: Saves stadium ID, name, location, capacity, condition.
      * kpfl_shop_inventory.txt: Saves item category, ID, name, unit price, stock.
      * kpfl_shop_receipts.txt: Saves receipt ID, customer name, subtotal, GCT, total paid.

6. THREE USEFUL MANAGEMENT REPORTS (SPECIFICATION SECTION 4.11)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system features three built-in management reports accessible via the menus:

REPORT 1: Top Goal Scorers (Golden Boot Race)
  - Scans all player records across every registered team.
  - Sorts players descending by goals scored.
  - Displays rank, player name, team, jersey number, and goal tally.

REPORT 2: Match Attendance & Stadium Capacity Utilization
  - Evaluates each fixture and stadium.
  - Computes capacity versus tickets sold.
  - Outputs percentage capacity occupancy for event safety and marketing analytics.

REPORT 3: Team Registration & Squad Status Summary
  - Summarizes each club's parish location, active squad size, and registration status
    (Active, Pending Approval, or Suspended).
